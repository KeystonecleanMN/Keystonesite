Home cleaning

Three ways to clean, each done top to bottom and room by room. Recurring cleans rotate in deeper tasks so nothing gets missed over time.

Standard clean

The regular upkeep that keeps your home feeling fresh week to week.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and appliance fronts
  • Bathrooms scrubbed and disinfected
  • Dusting, vacuuming and mopping
  • Beds made, trash out

Deep clean

A reset for homes that haven’t had a professional clean in a while.

  • Everything in a standard clean
  • Baseboards, doors and trim
  • Vents, ceiling fans and light fixtures
  • Grout, buildup and hard-water spots

Move-out clean

Get your deposit back or hand over the keys to a home that’s ready to show.

  • Everything in a deep clean
  • Inside empty cabinets and drawers
  • Inside oven and fridge
  • Closets, shelves and window sills

Booking and payment policies

Simple rules so your clean happens on time and our cleaners’ time is respected.

Payment

We save your card when you book and charge it after each clean, not before. Move-out and deep cleans need a 25% deposit when you book, and the rest is charged after the clean.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Cancel or reschedule for free with at least 48 hours’ notice. Cancellations with less than 48 hours’ notice are charged 50% of the clean.

Lockouts

If our cleaner can’t get into your home at the scheduled time, 50% of the clean is charged. Let us know ahead of time how to get in, such as a door code or lockbox.

Re-clean promise

If we miss something, tell us within 24 hours and we’ll come back and fix it at no charge. Because cleaning is a service, we make it right with a re-clean instead of a refund. Deposits are refunded if you cancel with at least 48 hours’ notice.

Commercial projects

Deposits, phase billing and payment terms are set out in your written bid before any work starts.

Questions

Email inquiries@keystonecleaningservicesmn.com about billing, scheduling or anything else.
